title: "Cordial 0.30.0: Hyprland camera drags hold, and recordings carry the game's sound"
---
## What you can do now

- **Holding right click on Hyprland keeps the cursor still** while the camera
  turns, and the cursor no longer reappears a title-bar height higher after each
  drag ([#56](https://github.com/luohoa97/cordial/issues/56)). If a drag ever
  sticks, `CORDIAL_DRAG_LOCK=0` goes back to 0.28.0's behaviour. Tested on
  Hyprland 0.56.2 with a virtual pointer, not yet with a real mouse.
- **Recordings carry the game's own sound**, not everything your desktop plays,
  so a call or music in another window stays out of the file. **F9** starts and
  stops a recording while the game has focus, and the window title shows
  `● REC` meanwhile. `CORDIAL_RECORD_KEY` changes the key or turns it off, and
  `CORDIAL_RECORD_AUDIO=desktop` keeps the old whole-desktop sound.
  ([#33](https://github.com/luohoa97/cordial/issues/33))
- **Typed text in multi-line boxes is drawn in the game's colour.** Under a
  light desktop theme it came out near-black, invisible on a dark game
  ([#86](https://github.com/luohoa97/cordial/issues/86)).
- **No crash at start on compositors without GPU buffer sharing.** Where the
  compositor does not offer `zwp_linux_dmabuf` (software-rendered sway,
  weston, cage), Vulkan can show nothing and Roblox could crash recording the
  error. Cordial now uses OpenGL ES there, which draws.
  `CORDIAL_FORCE_VULKAN_WITHOUT_DMABUF=1` keeps Vulkan.
  ([#63](https://github.com/luohoa97/cordial/issues/63))

## What is still broken

**On X11, typed text is not drawn at all.** Cordial's editor exists only on
Wayland, so on an X11 session what you type is sent but not shown.

**Recording has not captured a real game's sound yet.** It was tested with a
tone played through Cordial, signed out. F9 is also Roblox's developer-console
key on desktop; change it with `CORDIAL_RECORD_KEY` if you need that.

**The signed-in startup freeze.** Roblox can stop drawing on the loading screen
after sign-in. The launcher restarts a client stuck that way, up to twice.

**Movement keys can be dead after joining a game.** Move the mouse while the
place loads and it does not happen
([#29](https://github.com/luohoa97/cordial/issues/29)).

The rest of the list in the
[0.29.0 notes](https://github.com/luohoa97/cordial/releases/tag/v0.29.0) still
applies, except the start-up crash above.
