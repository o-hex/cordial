---
title: "Cordial 0.33.0: sticks move up and down, and two-step sign-in can finish"
---
## What you can do now

- **Controller sticks work in both directions.** Cordial sent each stick axis
  to Roblox on its own, but Roblox reads a stick as one event carrying both, so
  pushing a stick up or down moved things sideways. D-pad hats and triggers are
  fixed too, and a D-pad that is four buttons (the AYN Odin 3) now works.
  `CORDIAL_GAMEPAD_AXES` remaps axes for an unusual pad. Measured on Roblox's
  own menu with a simulated pad; the right stick and triggers are untested
  ([#113](https://github.com/luohoa97/cordial/issues/113)).
- **Holding left click and then right click no longer breaks the left click**
  ([#114](https://github.com/luohoa97/cordial/issues/114)).
- **Two-step verification at sign-in can report back.** The verification
  window now presents itself the way the Android app does, so Roblox's page
  talks to Cordial instead of staying silent until sign-in times out. Not yet
  tried with a real code ([#83](https://github.com/luohoa97/cordial/issues/83)).
- **Input stalls say where the time went** in the log
  ([#108](https://github.com/luohoa97/cordial/issues/108)).

## What is still broken

**The signed-in startup freeze.** Roblox can stop drawing on the loading screen
after sign-in. The launcher restarts a client stuck that way, up to twice
([#92](https://github.com/luohoa97/cordial/issues/92)).

**A startup crash on some machines** right after "app bridge initialised", on
NVIDIA, Intel and Steam Deck alike. We cannot reproduce it yet
([#109](https://github.com/luohoa97/cordial/issues/109)).

The rest of the list in the
[0.32.0 notes](https://github.com/luohoa97/cordial/releases/tag/v0.32.0) still
applies.
