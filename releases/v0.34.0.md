---
title: "Cordial 0.34.0: a fix for the signed-in startup freeze"
---
## What you can do now

- **The signed-in startup freeze should stop.** Roblox could stop drawing on
  the loading screen after sign-in when Cordial's settings call reached it just
  before its first render step. Cordial now waits for that step before making
  the call. Forcing the unsafe order on a loaded machine froze 2 of 2 launches
  without the wait and 0 of 3 with it; across all forced launches, 3 of 9 froze
  without it and 0 of 7 with it. That is one machine and too few launches to
  call it proven, so please report a freeze if you still see one
  ([#92](https://github.com/luohoa97/cordial/issues/92)).
  `CORDIAL_POST_GATE=0` turns the wait off.

## What is still broken

**If a freeze still happens**, the log will carry a `post gate:` line; please
include it and the lines around `Forcing finalize` from the Roblox log. A
freeze after a slow start (Roblox taking about a second to reach its home
screen) may have another cause.

**A startup crash on some machines** right after "app bridge initialised", on
NVIDIA, Intel and Steam Deck alike. We cannot reproduce it yet
([#109](https://github.com/luohoa97/cordial/issues/109)).

The rest of the list in the
[0.33.0 notes](https://github.com/luohoa97/cordial/releases/tag/v0.33.0) still
applies.
